# Attributions

## School information
Factual school information in `js/data.js` is derived from public RPS Public School Dharuhera and CBSE records. See `README.md` for source links.

## Visuals
The 3D campus geometry is procedurally generated for this digital experience and is explicitly presented as a visualization rather than an exact architectural replica.

No school photograph has been redistributed in this starter build. The UI links to the official school photo gallery/campus pages.
